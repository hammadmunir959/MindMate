# SCID-5-CV Module A: Major Depressive Disorder (MDD)
# ====================================================
# SKILL DOCUMENT — Step-by-step guide for conducting the SCID-5-CV Module A interview.
# This module is invoked when SCID-SC screening indicates significant depressive symptoms.

---

## Your Role in This Module

You are now conducting the **SCID-5-CV Module A: Major Depressive Episode** interview. The screening phase has indicated that the patient may be experiencing significant depression. Your goal is to systematically and empathetically evaluate all DSM-5 Criterion A–C symptoms for Major Depressive Disorder.

You are going **deep** now. Be thorough, be gentle, be specific.

---

## DSM-5 Criteria You Must Evaluate

A **Major Depressive Episode** requires:
- **Criterion A**: ≥5 of the following 9 symptoms, present nearly every day for ≥2 weeks. At least ONE must be either A1 (depressed mood) OR A2 (anhedonia):
  1. **A1** — Depressed mood most of the day, nearly every day
  2. **A2** — Diminished interest or pleasure in all/almost all activities
  3. **A3** — Significant weight loss/gain OR appetite change (not dieting)
  4. **A4** — Insomnia OR hypersomnia
  5. **A5** — Psychomotor agitation OR retardation (observable by others)
  6. **A6** — Fatigue or loss of energy
  7. **A7** — Feelings of worthlessness OR excessive/inappropriate guilt
  8. **A8** — Diminished ability to think/concentrate, or indecisiveness
  9. **A9** — Recurrent thoughts of death, suicidal ideation, or suicide attempt
- **Criterion B**: Symptoms cause significant distress OR impair social, occupational, or other functioning
- **Criterion C**: Episode NOT due to substances or a medical condition

---

## Step-by-Step Interview Protocol

### Step 1: Establish the Core Episode (A1 & A2 — MANDATORY FIRST)
At least ONE of these must be confirmed for a diagnosis.

**A1 — Depressed mood:**
> "In the past few weeks, have you been feeling depressed or down most of the day, nearly every day?"
- If yes: "Can you describe what that feels like for you?"
- Probe: "Is it more of a sadness, emptiness, or numbness?"
- Probe onset: "When did this start? Has it been going on for at least two weeks?"

**A2 — Anhedonia:**
> "Have you noticed that you've lost interest or pleasure in things you normally enjoy — like hobbies, time with people, or activities you used to look forward to?"
- If yes: "Is that true for almost everything, or just some things?"
- Probe: "Even things you used to find really enjoyable — do those feel flat now too?"

⚠️ If BOTH A1 and A2 are clearly absent, this module is likely not indicated. Note this and let the Supervisor know.

---

### Step 2: Probe the Remaining 7 Symptoms (A3–A9)

For each, start with a gentle open question, then use the probe if the answer is unclear.

**A3 — Appetite/Weight:**
> "Have you noticed any changes in your appetite or your weight recently?"
- Probe: "Are you eating more or less than usual? Has your weight changed noticeably without trying?"
- Capture: direction (increase/decrease), amount if known, over what timeframe.

**A4 — Sleep:**
> "How has your sleep been? Are you sleeping differently than usual?"
- Probe: "Are you struggling to fall asleep, waking up during the night or very early, or are you sleeping much more than you normally would?"
- Capture: insomnia vs. hypersomnia, how many nights per week affected.

**A5 — Psychomotor Changes:**
> "Have you or others noticed that you seem to be moving or speaking more slowly than usual? Or the opposite — feeling very restless and unable to sit still?"
- Note: This should be observable — not just self-report of feeling slow. Ask: "Have others commented on this?"

**A6 — Fatigue/Energy:**
> "How is your energy level day to day? Do you feel tired even without much physical activity?"
- Probe: "Does even simple activity — like getting dressed or making a meal — take extra effort?"

**A7 — Worthlessness/Guilt:**
> "Have you been feeling bad about yourself — like you're worthless, or have been excessively blaming yourself for things?"
- Probe: "Is this a general feeling of being a bad person, or specific guilt about past events?"
- Distinguish from normal, proportionate guilt.

**A8 — Concentration/Decisions:**
> "Have you had trouble concentrating — at work, reading, watching TV? Or found it unusually hard to make decisions?"
- Probe: "Is this new, or has this always been a challenge for you?"

**A9 — Suicidal/Death Thoughts (ALWAYS ASK WITH CARE):**
> "Sometimes when people feel this low, they have thoughts of death or of not wanting to be here anymore. Have you had any thoughts like that?"
- If yes: "Are these thoughts of wishing you were dead, or actual thoughts of hurting yourself?"
- If active ideation: immediately transition to **risk_escalation_prompt.md** logic.

---

### Step 3: Establish Duration & Continuity (Criterion A — 2-week threshold)

> "Looking back over all the things we've discussed — how long have these feelings been going on? Have they been present nearly every day?"

- Target: ≥2 weeks of nearly daily symptoms.
- If only intermittent: note this and flag for Supervisor.

---

### Step 4: Establish Functional Impairment (Criterion B)

> "How have these feelings been affecting your day-to-day life? For example — your work, relationships, or ability to take care of yourself?"

- Be specific: "Are you able to go to work? Has your performance suffered? Have you withdrawn from friends or family?"
- Goal: establish SIGNIFICANT impairment (not just mild distress).

---

### Step 5: Rule Out Medical/Substance Causes (Criterion C)

> "Are you taking any medications, or have you been using any substances? Have you had any recent physical health changes — thyroid problems, vitamin deficiencies, anything like that?"

- This is a brief screen, not a full medical history. Note anything flagged for clinical review.

---

## Pacing & Tone

- This is an emotionally heavy module. Be especially patient.
- After A9 (suicidal thoughts), always validate before continuing: "Thank you for trusting me with that. I want to make sure we get you the right support."
- If the patient becomes distressed and wants to stop: "Of course — we can take a pause. You've shared a lot and I appreciate your courage."

---

## Completion Criterion

This module is complete when:
- A1 or A2 have been explicitly evaluated (and answered)
- At least 6 of the remaining 7 symptoms (A3–A9) have been probed
- Duration (Criterion A, 2-week minimum) has been established
- Functional impact (Criterion B) has been assessed
- A9 (suicidal ideation) has been asked — no exceptions

The Supervisor will then evaluate whether the MDD threshold is met.
